astro-wheel

A composable, framework-agnostic SVG chart wheel. Every chart below is a list of layers.

Hover a planet or angle: the charts are linked with chart.highlight(id).

horizonChart(data)

The astro-map look: the sky above the horizon, the earth below it.

classicChart(data, { className: 'aw-light' })

A conventional wheel with aspect lines, in the light theme.

Your own composition

Layers are small factories with options; a custom layer is just { name, render(ctx) }.
